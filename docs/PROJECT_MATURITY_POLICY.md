# Project maturity policy

Reviewed 2026-10-08. The evidence register is `portfolio/maturity.json`; project READMEs and website descriptions must not claim a stronger grade without updating evidence here.

- **Experimental:** exploration or incomplete verification; no dependable user workflow established.
- **Alpha:** at least one implemented, reproducible workflow and checks; significant limitations remain.
- **Beta:** supported installation and published artifacts, tested primary workflows, documented limitations and recovery; independent user validation remains incomplete.
- **Stable:** repeatable releases and upgrades, supported platforms, regression coverage of primary workflows, demonstrated recovery, security and license boundaries, and recorded sustained real-user validation.

Version v1.0, successful CI, screenshots and marketing labels do not independently establish Stable. Missing evidence is a limitation, not evidence of absence. Grades here are conservative assessments of linked records, not a new certification.

Every entry records a source commit, dated evidence links, known limitations, unmet conditions and next-grade requirements. Review after substantial releases or changes in supported workflows. Website content may reference this register, but must retain separate research state and content category fields.
